# Magic and Awakening

[Design summary](README.md) · Status: designed; Magic can be raised as latent talent, casting skills are not built

Every character starts at Magic 0 and can raise it with Karma from creation onward. On its own, Magic is **latent talent**: it does nothing until the character knows a **casting skill**, a tradition's foundation skill. Knowing one is what it means to be Awakened, and which one decides the character's tradition.

- **Awakening is part of the skill system.** There is no separate Awakened state: a character is Awakened when they know a foundation skill, so the usual routes for acquiring skills (starting skills, teaching, epiphany) are the routes to Awakening.
- **Foundation skills require Magic 1 or more.** Latent talent is what makes a character eligible to Awaken later; spending Karma on Magic before Awakening is an investment, not a waste.
- **Other magic skills require the tradition's foundation skill.** A spell's effectiveness is its skill level plus Magic (see [Karma](attributes.md#karma) for raising Magic).
- **Casting has a cost, Drain,** resisted by Willpower plus the tradition's attribute.

## How to Awaken

1. **At character creation,** through an Awakened background such as Acolyte or Mystic. These backgrounds require Magic 1 or more, bought with starting Karma, and grant the tradition's foundation skill (see [Backgrounds](characters.md#backgrounds)). Without one, any Magic bought at creation stays latent.
2. **A tradition's self-taught path:** the foundation skill is a [hidden skill](skills.md#hidden-skills) learned by epiphany when the path's conditions are met. See the table below.
3. **Apprenticeship:** a master teaches the foundation skill. They must have mastered it, the same rule as teaching a loot skill. The apprentice needs only the skill's requirements, starting with Magic 1. There is no limit on apprentices: the time it takes to reach mastery is the limit.
4. **Ordeal:** surviving a near-death event with high Willpower and latent Magic. The epiphany grants the foundation skill of the tradition matching the character's strongest tradition attribute.

## Traditions

| Tradition | Archetype | Self-taught path | Tradition attribute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hermetic | Magician | **Study:** high Logic, a lore skill, and reading a grimoire in a particular library | Logic |
| Shamanic | Shaman, druid | **Communion:** high Intuition and nights alone in wild places until a spirit answers | Charisma |
| Divine | Cleric | **Devotion:** high Willpower and prayer at shrines until a power answers | Intuition |
| Adept | Monk, warrior-mystic | **Discipline:** high Willpower and Body, and pushing a physical skill to its limit | Body |

The tradition decides which magic trees open: adepts get physical-magic trees (enhanced reflexes, killing hands), while hermetics, shamans and clerics get spellcasting trees with different flavours. Self-taught paths can also test latent Magic, for example Study requiring Magic 2, so early investment makes Awakening easier.

## Which paths are known

**Study is public; the rest are rumours.** The library and its grimoire are a known quest, slow but certain, so no one who wants to play a magic user is locked out. The other self-taught paths are hidden epiphanies that players piece together.

## Possible later additions

- **Essence:** dark pacts or iron grafts give power now and lower Magic for good.
- **Burnout:** very rare events reduce Magic.
