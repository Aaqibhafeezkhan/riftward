A handoff note for picking the work back up: what's built, what's next, and what's waiting on a decision. The design docs hold the detail; this page points into them.

## What's built

Riftward is a classless telnet MUD on headless Bevy 0.20. Everything below is committed, tested live on a scratch server, and documented.

- **Engine:** telnet with proper negotiation (`src/telnet.rs`), prompts marked with EOR or GA for MUD clients, one command per tick, an action queue (actions take a 3-second round in a fight, a second out of one), skill practice paced so out of a fight it grows no faster than in one, game events, a game clock (a day every 3 real hours), a one-second heartbeat.
- **World:** zone files (RON, hot-reloaded) for Riftwatch (26 rooms), its sewers (23) and the Darkwood (56: the road, the west woods, the ravine, the deep wood, the standing stones and the Rift-Scar); doors and locks; terrain that costs stamina to cross; in-game building.
- **Items:** templates, Bulk and encumbrance, hands and wear slots, containers and automatic placement, weapons, corpses that rot, real items that survive restarts, item spawns, unique items.
- **Mobs:** templates and spawns (keeping a count, breeding, after-death and reseed timers, gated by world flags), wandering and crowding, crowds breaking through closed doors (rats gnawing through the grates), pack limits, aggression, auras, the players' vitals, technique skills used by chance, and equipment they fight with and drop; and [mobs fighting mobs](world.md#mobs-fighting-mobs): tags, allies and enemies on templates, so the guards and the stray dog fight the rats in a breakout.
- **Characters:** creation (race with attribute shifts, starting Karma, a background as a past life, a description), vitals that recover (faster resting, offline too, never in a fight), `score`, `describe`, aliases, a custom prompt.
- **Skills:** trees as data (`assets/skills/*.skill.ron`), use-based progression with practice and gain cooldowns, uncapped levels with a soft cap from character level, experience and character levels that earn Karma, opposed-roll contests, active and passive skills, attacks, techniques, auras, passive stat bonuses, counters (Riposte), trainers (`learn`, entry skills only), discovery of a tree's other skills through use, a tough-fight boost to learning, Awakening to the four magic traditions.
- **Karma:** attributes cost the rating they reach (3 to 4 costs 4); characters start with 20 (humans 32); `karma <skill>` unlocks a priced skill in a tree the character has, once its requirements are met.
- **Characters:** players and mobs share commands, output and rules. Mobs can have races; wandering mobs walk with `go`, and aggressive ones start fights with `kill`; GMs can `puppet` a mob and play it.
- **Roles:** player, builder, GM and admin, with builders and GMs side by side: builders edit the world's files, GMs run live events (puppeting, summoning, world flags).
- **Conditions** are data (`assets/conditions/`): twenty-one, made of modifiers, with their own wording and hints; techniques, spells and items can apply or grant any of them, and timed ones are saved. A condition can also be put on one item (`Imbue`): its modifiers pass to whoever holds it and its `on_hit` effects ride on the weapon's blows, for a time or a number of hits, saved with the item.
- **Effects** ([effects.md](effects.md)): damage types as data (`physical`, the elements, the energies, light, shadow, force, poison), armour that stops only what it says, `Resist`, `Weakness` and `Immune`; one effect vocabulary for spells and techniques (`Damage`, `Heal`, `Apply`, `Remove`, `Cure`, `Imbue`, `Pierce`, `Cleave`…); one resolver for blows, techniques and spells, each naming what resists it (Grapple is against Strength); passive skills that resist by tag, starting with the elves' Fey Mind against sleep and charm; and formulas, so every amount in an effect is written in data (`"roll(2, 5) + floor(power / 3)"`); and reactions, effects that conditions and passive skills set off by themselves, in a fight (thorns tearing at an attacker) or out of one (entering a room, someone arriving, resting, eating, a condition taking hold or running out, every so often): poison leaves its bearer weakened as it runs its course. Active skills can be made of effects too: First Aid is a heal and a cure, against how bad the wound is, using up a bandage.
- **Items** also have quality (crude to masterwork, per item) and rarity (common to legendary, per template), which set their worth and, for quality, a weapon's damage or armour's protection.
- **Money and shops:** a copper, silver and gold purse, coins from kills and from mobs' pockets, three Riftwatch shops that sell, buy and resell, spell lessons that cost money, and the Trade tree: Haggling (a deal with each shopkeeper) and Appraisal.
- **Spells:** spell books as data (`assets/spells/`), `cast` and `spells` (`use` finds spells too), Drain resisted by Willpower, the first two spells of each casting tradition and Hermetic's Kindle Blade, taught by each tradition's teacher, cast in or out of fights by players and mobs alike.
- **Combat:** rounds in initiative order, auto-attacks, weapons (trained and untrained), techniques for every weapon tree's journeyman tier, conditions (staggered, down, bleeding, held, guarding, exposed), stamina costs, fleeing, death and respawn at the shrine, corpses that keep a player's gear, and death for anyone who disconnects mid-fight (`quit` refuses in one).
- **Communication:** `say`, `emote`, `whisper`, `yell` and `gossip`.
- **World events:** saved world flags and data-driven events. A rat breakout into the city raises the Rat King, whose aura toughens the rats until he dies.

- **Project:** public at [Foxworks-Studios/riftward](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward), dual-licensed MIT or Apache-2.0. CI checks formatting, clippy and tests on every push and pull request, and [issues](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues) are labelled for contributors (`good first issue`, `no rust needed`, `playtesting`…); several items below link to theirs.

The [README](README.md) has the fuller list, and each design doc says what of it is built.

## Next up

**Growing the world before scripting** (decided 2026-10-27): content shows what data can't express, so step 6 ([scripting](effects.md#scripting)) is designed against real needs, not guesses. First [mobs fighting mobs](world.md#mobs-fighting-mobs) and reinforcements (a losing guard calls for help; both built), then the Darkwood and its fauna ([design](darkwood.md): about 60 rooms, built as data where it can be, with the gaps that need code listed). The low tiers come first ([the ladder](darkwood.md#the-ladder-from-the-sewers-to-the-deep-wood)): training dummies in town (built: `inanimate` mobs; and for what's next, mobs born with conditions, `avoids`, `Spawn` with a `Dies` trigger, `Flee` with skittish prey, and [traits](effects.md#traits), what a creature or race *is* (skittish, inanimate, the elves' Fey Mind), all built), [the sewers' ecology](riftwatch.md#the-sewers-ecology) of slimes and small creatures (built: oozes that split, grey slimes, the Slime Lord, cockroaches, a snake and a rat swarm; slimes leave jelly, not corpses), and [the west woods](darkwood.md#the-west-woods-as-built) (built: fifteen rooms of prey, boars, frogs, a badger, a stag and the hunter's camp), so there's something between the rats and the deep wood; keeping a list of what couldn't be done in data. Step 5 is built: active skills can be made of effects in data, and First Aid is (5a, [as built](effects.md#active-skills-as-built)); Lockpicking too, aimed at a lock, with `locked` as a condition (5b), Foraging and Herbalism, aimed at the room, with `Find` (5c), Pickpocketing, with `Take` (5d), and Appraisal and Tracking, with `Reveal` (5e); their old commands are shortcuts. `hidden` is a condition now, and Hiding is data too, so [every skill is data](effects.md#every-skill-as-data); and mobs use active skills in a fight by chance, as they do techniques and spells.  Built so far: reactions out of a fight (4b: entering a room, someone arriving, resting, eating and drinking, a condition taking hold or running out, casting, the start of a turn, and `Every(Minutes(n))`), reactions in a fight (4a: conditions and passive skills react when their bearer hits, is struck, dodges, kills, dies or is badly hurt, as [thorns](effects.md#reactions-as-built) do), formulas (3: every amount in an effect is a [formula](effects.md#formulas-as-built) in data, `"roll(2, 5) + floor(power / 3)"`), conditions as data (1), damage types (2a: typed weapons, attacks and spells, armour that says what it stops, `Resist`, `Weakness` and `Immune`), one effect vocabulary (2b), skills that resist by tag (2c-i), and one resolver for spells, techniques and blows, so either can use any effect and any contest (2c-ii), and conditions on single items: `Imbue`, `on_hit`, charges, saved with the item (2d).

## Small things noticed and not yet done

- Fight balance has a tool now: `cargo test balance_ -- --ignored --nocapture --test-threads 1` fights parties built for character levels 0 to 10 against whole encounters, a report for each place (`balance_town`, `balance_sewer_rats`, `balance_sewer_slimes`, `balance_deep_wood`). The deep wood is tuned with it ([Tuned against parties](darkwood.md#tuned-against-parties), 2026-10-29), and the sewers, both sides, against new characters ([Tuned against new characters](riftwatch.md#tuned-against-new-characters), 2026-10-30), with Crooked Alley's alley rats added off the Back Alley as a new character's first fights. Health became 8 + Body + Toughness, stamina 8 + Willpower + Endurance, a whole point for every point and level (2026-10-30, [#13](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues/13)): about 26 at level 6 and 32 at 10, and the tier 2 and higher mobs and the guards retuned to match. Bigger parties win more surely than the targets say, as they should, since a fight rated as a whole pays them less (decided 2026-10-30). Findings waiting: the west woods aren't checked yet.
- Attacks use the margin as blows (glancing to crushing), and so does gathering for the quality of what's found; quiet picks don't yet. Later Stealth and Knife skills could add Backstab damage, or make crushing blows easier for it.
- Experience and level numbers are first guesses: 50 for an even fight, nothing below a one-in-ten risk, 1,000 a level, 5 Karma a level. With levels no longer slowing on their own, the soft cap (10 + 2 × level) and how high levels go both depend on how hard the world's fights get, which needs watching as content grows.
- Difficulties are re-rated for the steeper contests and still first guesses: climbs and swims 4 (about one try in three untrained), ordinary locks 4, foraging 3.
- A calmed animal stays calm until it dies or resets: there's no time limit yet ([#5](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues/5)).
- Spells come only from teachers: scrolls and grimoires, a spell-holding limit, more schools and great spells costing health are still to come. An admin can know every tradition's foundation; players can't yet, an open question.
- Spell numbers are first guesses: Drain 2 or 3, resisted at twice that, power = school level + its attribute + Magic, a third of which adds to damage, healing and wards. A teacher at school 20 with Magic 4 casts a Spark or Ward that a new character can't match.
- Races are uneven at the new Karma prices (shifts worth 14 to 22 Karma, humans 12 and Versatility), left until racial skills are built and each race can be balanced as a whole ([Races](characters.md#races)). The first racial skill exists, the elves' Fey Mind (resisting sleep and charm), as an example of skills resisting tags; it adds to their package unbalanced. The rest, Versatility included, are still to come.
- Karma numbers are first guesses: 20 starting (32 for humans), 5 a level, and skill prices from 6 to 16.
- Quality odds and multipliers are first guesses (half of found things standard, masterwork ×3 and +2), and so are the rarity multipliers.
- Haggling's numbers are first guesses: deals of a twentieth to a fifth, lasting ten minutes, and shopkeepers at 5 to 10. Deals aren't saved, and a deal is only struck by those who know Haggling.
- Prices are first guesses (see [Prices](economy.md#prices)), and so is buying at a third. A shop's resale stock and a player's money on death are open questions.
- Puppeting is for GMs and admins (builders can't). `help` still lists GM commands under "Building" and "Admin" ([#4](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues/4)). While puppeting, the builder hears nothing of their own body, even if it's attacked. Lessons (`learn`) and account commands (`raise`, `alias`, `describe`) are for players only.
- Conditions: Nourished and Hidden are data conditions now. Modifiers beyond the fifteen built (initiative, attributes, blind, fear, haste…) come when something needs them. Little content uses the newer conditions yet (envenomed blades poison, nothing casts sleep); designers can, with `Apply`, `Imbue` and `grants`.
- Item conditions can't yet change an item's own stats (rust), make it impossible to remove (curses), or go on armour.
- Mobs hide and ambush, and `search` finds hidden doors (2026-10-29, [#9](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues/9)); no zone has a hidden door yet, and a searching skill doesn't add to the roll.
- Hiding isn't saved: logging out shows you, and a restart forgets everyone's hiding.
- Rat tails, herbs, the Rat King's tooth and the heart of the deep are all asked for by contracts now. Bosses (`boss: true`) always drop what someone on their quest still needs; rats' tails stay at their 30%.
- Foraging's patch numbers are first guesses: three finds a room, one back every 10 minutes.
- First Aid's numbers are first guesses too: a novice (Intuition 3) heals 2 to 4, and treats a badly hurt patient about a third of the time.
- Waterskins can't be drunk from; they need refilling, so they wait for wells and rivers ([#10](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues/10)).
- Armour's numbers are first guesses. Guards in studded leather and a helm (3 armour) shrug off most of a new character's punches.
- The tough-fight odds count armour's penalty and a shield's block, but not its reduction, so a heavily armoured foe counts as less tough than it is ([#8](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues/8)).
- Town guards drop their short swords and armour and respawn, and the tinker buys anything: a guard is worth about 1 gold 6 silver in gear, plus his coins. Worth a look in play ([open question](economy.md#open-questions)).
- Discovery chance (0.1% a use) and the tough-fight cap (2×) are first guesses, untested in play.
- Room descriptions don't show who's fighting whom ([#2](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues/2)).
- A pack or herd gets one line per animal ("The sewer rat attacks you!" once per rat), DikuMUD's way, kept as the default (decided 2026-10-27); an opt-in `combine` setting to fold them is [#3](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues/3).
- `pick` doesn't require lockpicks, though urchins carry them ([#1](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues/1)).
- Some backgrounds' skills have no tree yet: Animal Handling, Smithing, Scribing, Lore, Performance and Sailing, so those backgrounds start without them. They're granted at login once the trees are written ([#11](https://github.com/Foxworks-Studios/riftward/issues/11)).
- The rat breakout has only been tested by setting the flag by hand (`worldflag`), not by a rat wandering up through an open grate.
- Expert and master tiers of every tree, Archery and Shields.
- Weather turning ground to mud is designed ([Weather](world.md#weather-planned)), not built.

## Working on it

- **Checks on every change:** `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy --all-targets` and `cargo test` must be clean. Commit only when asked, with design docs updated alongside the code.
- **Testing live:** run a scratch server, never the real database:

  ```
  MUD_DB=/tmp/scratch.db MUD_ADDR=127.0.0.1:4001 cargo run
  ```

  Then script a session with `nc 127.0.0.1 4001` (end it with `quit` twice, once to leave the character and once at the menu, or `nc` waits forever), or a small Python socket client for things `nc` can't do, like dropping the connection. The first account made on a fresh database is an admin.
- **Builder and admin commands rewrite zone files.** Back up `assets/zones` before a test that uses them, and restore it after.
- **Admin commands for testing:** `grantskill`, `setattr`, `setvitals`, `condition` (put any condition on someone here or online, mobs too: `condition guard thorns 3 5t`), `worldflag`, `summon`, `purge`, `goto`, `create`. `create` and `summon` take a template's id or the words it answers to (`summon dog`, `create short sword fine`), this zone first, then every zone; if several match, they're listed by id.
- **Stopping a test server:** stop it by the process listening on its port (`lsof -t -iTCP:4001 -sTCP:LISTEN`), never by name, so a real server on 4000 is left alone.
- **Deterministic combat tests** build a tiny world and run one `combat_round` (see the tests in `src/combat/mod.rs`), which beats chasing dice live.
